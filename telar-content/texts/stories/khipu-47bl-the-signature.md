---
title: "The Signature"
---

Although often made of cotton, camelid fibers, and other organic materials, there is one specific fiber that is used in the making of the khipu. Used to add a certain signature into the counting tool, human hair was proven to be found within khipus. The strands of human hair are added by whoever created the khipu, an unseen yet present way that they claimed their work. As a result, researchers have been able to make certain observations on who exactly used the khipu and different information about them through their DNA. This involves their sex, hair color, genetic makeup, and age.
