---
title: "Khipus in the Inca World"
---

Khipus were used throughout the Inca Empire, which stretched across much of western South America. The empire's capital was Cusco, but its people lived across mountains, deserts, and valleys separated by vast distances. To govern this diverse territory, the Inca developed systems for managing information and connecting communities. Khipus became an important part of this process and were used throughout the empire.

Although khipus originated in the Andes centuries ago, they can still be found today in museums and university collections. The University of California, Santa Barbara houses its own khipu, giving students and researchers the opportunity to study this unique object firsthand and connect with the history of the Andes.
