---
title: "Khipus in the Inca World"
---

Khipus were used throughout the Inca Empire, known as Tawantinsuyu, which stretched across present-day Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador, Chile, Argentina, and Colombia. Governing such a vast territory required ways to organize information and connect communities separated by mountains, deserts, and great distances. Khipus became an important part of this system and were used across the empire.

Today, khipus can be found in museums, archaeological collections, and universities around the world. The University of California, Santa Barbara houses its own khipu, allowing students and researchers to study this remarkable object firsthand. The presence of a khipu at UCSB demonstrates how these artifacts continue to connect people with the history and culture of the Andes.
