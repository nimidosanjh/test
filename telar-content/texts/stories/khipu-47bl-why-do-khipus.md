---
title: "Why Do Khipus Matter?"
---

Khipus are important because they reveal how the Inca created and preserved knowledge. Unlike many civilizations that relied on alphabetic writing systems, the Inca developed their own methods for recording, organizing, and communicating information. As a result, khipus have become central to modern efforts to understand Inca society and Indigenous systems of knowledge.

This website explores the many dimensions of the khipu. The following sections examine how khipus were made, how they functioned, how colors contributed to their meaning, and who created and used them. Together, these topics show that khipus were far more than bundles of knotted cords—they were sophisticated tools that helped connect people, information, and communities across the Inca world.
