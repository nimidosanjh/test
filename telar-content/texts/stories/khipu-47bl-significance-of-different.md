---
title: "Significance of different colors "
---

It was recently discovered that different names of tributaries in Inka culture might have been coded into Khipu lines. Specifically in the Santa Valley Khipus, there were thirty different colors which may have corresponded with the census which recorded around thirty names in the region. Additionally, sources suggest that colors may have also designated certain societal roles. One source suggested the different bands of color linked to Moiety divisions; A moiety system is a dual organization within the Inka culture that is determined from birth. An individual is designated a certain group (Hanan or Hurin), which may indicate a geographical region where that group lives or might also indicate what political roles they are involved in. Within these Hanan and Hurin groups are Ayullus which are ethnic groups. Another study showed that the Khipu colors may have also indicated different tasks within Ayullus. It was found there were six different tasks assigned to an individual, indicated by color but also by the different knots tied at the ends.
